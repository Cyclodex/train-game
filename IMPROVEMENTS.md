## How the game plays today

- Levels are **data**, not hardcoded: `src/levels/default.ts` plus anything built
  in `/editor` or generated by `tiles/generate.ts`, handed over via
  `src/levelStore.ts`. `/test` hosts 75 isolated feature scenarios.
- Tiles carry **terrain** (`TileCell.terrain?`: forest/water/rock/mountain/urban,
  organic patch rendering, derived scatter) and water/rock/mountain **block
  building** through one predicate (`canBuildOn`, `src/tiles/terrain.ts`).
- The world is **editable while the sim runs** (`game.applyEdits`/`canEdit`:
  occupied/reserved tiles rejected, switch arms merged, `levelVersion` bumped) —
  which is what makes an in-play build tool possible at all.
- An authoritative headless simulation (`src/sim/*`) advances trains on a
  deterministic tick, with **path reservation / interlocking**, **momentum**
  (mass-derived accel/brake), signals, depots and level crossings. The renderer
  (`src/game.ts` + a rAF loop) only draws the sampled state.
- A parallel **road simulation** carries cars/trucks/buses on directed lanes with
  lane changes, overtaking, lane-aware routing, junction arbitration, junction
  traffic signals and bus lanes.
- A **game-modes framework** (`src/modes/`) layers objectives over the same sim:
  sandbox, puzzle, daily, time attack, crossing keeper and **tycoon** (money,
  waiting trains, decaying fares, build-for-cash) — with stars, fail predicates
  and per-level best-result persistence (`src/objectiveStore.ts`).
- Depot/train colours are **deterministic and solvable** (`utils/colorAssignment.ts`,
  seeded by `gameConfig.colorSeed`), not random.

**The biggest gap is no longer the objective loop — it is that only one mode
(crossing keeper) scores the road layer at all.** See
`docs/road-future-improvements.md` §1.

**Worlds are no longer capped** (2026-07-25). A board is as big as its content
(`src/tiles/bounds.ts`), all three views share a camera (pan/zoom/fit,
`src/camera.ts` + `src/cameraController.ts`), and the editor grows by drawing into
a margin — with ⬅︎+ / ⬆︎+ to extend before the origin, since the engine anchors the
world at 0,0. `demoworld` (20×14, rail ring + street grid + 8 level crossings) is
the demo, playable at `/#/play?board=demoworld`.

**Terrain answered that** (2026-07-26): the world's ground is tile data now, and
the first rule (unbuildable water/rock/mountain) is what turned the build tool
into a puzzle. What remains of terrain is rules, not data — see items 1 and 6.

## Gameplay

1. **Finish the Tycoon mode** (the active thread). The loop works; the ordered
   remainder — annual tax + calendar clock (what gives the money bite), goals on
   the Ready card, clearing costs + buildable plots, destination badges, briefing
   screen, campaign, player-called trains, the road layer joining the economy —
   lives in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-25-train-valley-mode-design.md` §8,
   sized and sequenced. The first two are S each and are the next commits.
   Two loose ends from the same work: `generateLevel` and `demoworld` paint no
   terrain yet, so generated/daily boards and `/play`'s demo are still bare grass.
2. **A road-first game mode**. "Keep the city flowing": move N cars through the
   map inside a time limit without any car waiting too long. The counters
   (`maxCarWaitSec`, `carsDelivered`) and the objective state machine already
   exist in `src/sim/objectives.ts`; `gameConfig.roadScoring` is wired but barely
   exercised. Needs `src/modes/traffic-manager.ts`, HUD wiring and a `/test`
   scenario. Detail: `docs/road-future-improvements.md` §1.1.
3. **Stations as through-track stops** (biggest design unlock). A station tile
   with a queue, `TrainStatus.Dwelling`, boarding logic and a platform render.
   Unlocks typed cargo (replacing colour matching), endless/management mode, and
   lets road traffic feed rail. `docs/road-future-improvements.md` §2.1.
4. **Deadlock resolution.** Interlocking prevents collisions, but two trains can
   still reserve into a mutual wait. Needs cycle detection over the reservation
   graph plus a backout rule. `src/sim/simulation.ts`, `src/sim/network.ts`.
5. **Signaling phase 2/3**: pre-signals / yellow aspects and speed signals (the
   momentum prerequisite is done), then path-based signaling.
   `docs/signaling-design.md` lists what was deferred.
6. **Bridges + tunnels.** Blocking terrain shipped (`canBuildOn`); what remains
   is the exception to it: a `bridge` role with two independent port-pairs so
   road can cross rail (or rail cross water) without a level crossing — the
   connection model already supports two non-interacting pairs on one cell, and
   a bridge/tunnel is designed as an *exception inside `canBuildOn`*, not a
   second rule. `docs/road-future-improvements.md` §3.1.
7. **Polish.** Sound effects (depart, brake, deliver, crash), richer arrival
   feedback, and pixel-uniform wagon coupling (spacing is measured in tile
   fractions, so couplings read slightly tighter on curves than on straights).

## Road traffic

0. **PARKING — the next four pieces**, written up with the measurements that are
   their acceptance criteria: `docs/handoff-parking-next.md`. The pivot-arc
   reverse (which unblocks reverse-parking for 90°/echelon bays), right of way
   when leaving a bay, a slower reversing speed, and the echelon apron that
   overhangs its tile. Take them in that order — the first two are the ones the
   feature is actually waiting on.

8. **Oncoming-lane overtaking** on 1-lane-each-way roads — the distance/speed
   feasibility math plus abort. Same-direction overtaking is done. Spec:
   `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-06-07-overtaking-driver-behaviour-design.md` §3b.
9. **Per-road speed limits.** Cars already carry `speed` and `cornerCap`; a
   per-tile/per-road cap makes the effective speed `min(preferred, road limit,
   car ahead)`. Small, and it gives level authors a pacing dial.
10. **Driver profiles** (aggressive/cautious, truck slowdown variance) — adds life,
   low priority.
11. **Per-`(tile, lane)` route planner.** Routes are tile sequences with lane
    sorting on approach; a true lane-cost planner would handle dense turn-lane
    networks more robustly. Optimisation, not a blocker.
12. **Paid / time-limited parking.** Parked out until the Tycoon ledger existed —
    "there is no money in this game and nothing to spend it on" — which stopped
    being true when `sim/economy.ts` landed. Now a real option: a per-facility
    tariff, a fee charged on `resumeFromStall` (the one place a stay ends), and a
    time limit that makes a driver leave early or overstay. The parking side needs
    almost nothing: `ParkingCell` already carries a per-facility `dwellSec`, and a
    `tariff` sits beside it. The work is deciding what the player DOES with the
    money — a fee nobody chooses to set is a number on a sign, not a mechanic.
    Files: `src/tiles/parking.ts`, `src/sim/parking.ts`, `src/sim/economy.ts`.

## Architecture / code health

12. **Sass `@use` migration.** Stylesheets still use `@import` (deprecation is
    silenced in `vite.config.ts`); migrate `src/scss/global/*` to the module
    system before it becomes a hard error.
13. **ESLint config cleanup.** Both a legacy `.eslintrc.js` and a flat
    `eslint.config.js` exist on ESLint 9 — the legacy file is dead weight and
    `npm run lint` should be verified clean in CI.
14. **Type the loose `any`s** in the routing/lane code (`src/sim/*`,
    `src/tiles/*`) — catches real bugs as the sim grows.
15. **Fix the "fraight" typo** in one sweep (type strings + asset names), or leave
    it — but do it all at once, never piecemeal.
16. **Longer term**: migrate the class components to `<script setup>` +
    composables now that a Vitest/Playwright safety net exists. This removes the
    `vue-facing-decorator` inheritance machinery but is a large, careful refactor.

## Recently landed (kept for context)

- The **model/view refactor**: an authoritative deterministic simulation with
  headless unit tests; collisions are impossible (a train never enters an occupied
  tile) and trains never move on a red signal.
- **Signaling phase 1**: block/route reservation (interlocking), always-visible
  directional aspects, manual hold. `docs/signaling-design.md`.
- **Train momentum**: velocity, look-ahead braking, mass-derived rates.
  `src/sim/physics.ts`.
- **Level loading + editor**: levels are data, the editor authors track *and*
  roads (lane counts, turn restrictions, signals), with validation and import/export.
- **The whole road layer** (directed lanes → multi-lane → turn rules → vehicle
  classes → lane-aware routing → lane changing → overtaking → junction signals).
  `docs/road-network-progress.md`.
- **Game modes + objectives framework**, deterministic colour assignment, world
  themes, and the `/test` feature gallery.
- **Unbounded worlds + a camera** (2026-07-25), the `demoworld` board, and two
  registry-wide guards: `npm run probe` (render-level: tile placement, mismatch
  paint, console errors, merge-arrow direction across every registry scenario)
  and `tests/unit/sim/roadScenarioSweep.spec.ts` (behavioural: every road
  scenario populates, flows and never clips). Both were written after finding
  bugs by eye that neither the unit tests nor a screenshot would have caught.
- **Terrain as tile data** (2026-07-26): `TileCell.terrain?` with organic,
  neighbour-agreeing patch outlines and derived scatter; `canBuildOn` as the one
  build-blocking predicate. `src/tiles/terrain.ts`.
- **The Tycoon mode, end to end** (2026-07-26): live level editing under a
  running sim (`game.applyEdits`), a pure economy ledger + decaying fare book
  (`src/sim/economy.ts`), waiting trains with click-to-dispatch, an in-play
  build tool with live cost preview (`routeDrawController.ts` +
  `game.buildRoute`), and `lakevalley-open` — Train Valley level 1 as a playable
  level with three verified, mutually-tensioned goals.
